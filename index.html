Skymerge — Privacy Policy

EnglishTürkçe

1. Who we are

Skymerge (Android package com.erakarstudio.skymerge, "the Game") is developed by erakarstudio. If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact us at eraykaraer0234@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • We do not run our own servers and we do not collect, sell or share personal data ourselves.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device.
  • The Game uses Google services that process some data: Google AdMob (ads), Google Play Billing (in-app purchases) and, if you sign in, Google Play Games Services (leaderboards, achievements, cloud save).
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs other than the ones listed here.
  • The Game is not directed at children under 13.

3. Data stored on your device

Your gems, scores, settings, language, nickname, chosen country flag, purchases you made and puzzle progress are saved locally on your device so the Game works offline. This data does not leave your device, except for the optional cloud save described below. Reminders (notifications) are scheduled locally on your device; we do not use a push notification server.

4. Advertising — Google AdMob

The Game shows ads (banner, interstitial, app open and optional rewarded ads) served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect and process: the advertising ID of your device, device information (model, operating system, language, screen), your IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), app interactions with ads (views, clicks), and diagnostic data. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time in the Game: Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads). Ad requests are sent with a content rating limit (PG) and are not tagged as child-directed.

After any in-app purchase, interstitial and app open ads are paused for 7 days. Players who buy "Remove Ads", have an active Premium subscription or have spent at least the equivalent of US$4.99 in total no longer see banner, interstitial and app open ads. More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. In-app purchases — Google Play Billing

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details. To deliver what you bought, the Game receives the product ID, an order/transaction ID and a purchase token from Google Play and keeps a record of processed purchases on your device.

6. Google Play Games Services (optional)

If you sign in with Google Play Games, Google provides the Game with your Play Games player ID, display name and avatar so that leaderboards and achievements work. Your scores and achievements are stored by Google. If you use cloud save, a copy of your game progress (the data listed in section 3, without payment data) is stored in your Google account through Play Games "Saved Games". You can sign out in the Game at any time.

7. Analytics

The Game does not include analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs. Google Play may provide us with aggregated, anonymous statistics (for example installs and crash rates) under Google's own policies.

8. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the Game, contact us and we will help to delete it.

9. Security

The Google SDKs used by the Game transmit data over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

10. Your choices and data deletion

  • Local data: uninstall the Game or use Android Settings → Apps → Skymerge → Storage → Clear data.
  • Cloud save / Play Games data: delete it from your Google Play Games settings, or email us and we will delete the cloud save for you.
  • Advertising data: manage it with the in-game privacy options and your Android advertising ID settings; Google's data controls are at myaccount.google.com.
  • Deletion request: send an email to eraykaraer0234@gmail.com with the subject "Skymerge data deletion". We answer within 30 days.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

12. Contact

erakarstudio — eraykaraer0234@gmail.com

4. Reklamlar — Google AdMob

Oyun, Google AdMob tarafından sunulan reklamlar gösterir (banner, geçiş, açılış ve isteğe bağlı ödüllü reklamlar). Reklamları sunmak, ölçmek ve sahtekârlığı önlemek için AdMob SDK'sı şu verileri toplayabilir ve işleyebilir: cihazınızın reklam kimliği, cihaz bilgileri (model, işletim sistemi, dil, ekran), yaklaşık konumu gösterebilen IP adresi, reklamlarla etkileşimler (görüntüleme, tıklama) ve tanılama verileri. AEA, Birleşik Krallık ve İsviçre'de kişiselleştirilmiş reklamlardan önce Google'ın Kullanıcı Mesajlaşma Platformu (UMP) ile onayınız istenir; tercihinizi Oyun'da Ayarlar → Gizlilik Seçenekleri bölümünden istediğiniz zaman değiştirebilirsiniz. Reklam kimliğinizi Android ayarlarından sıfırlayabilir veya silebilirsiniz (Ayarlar → Google → Reklamlar / Gizlilik → Reklamlar). Reklam istekleri içerik sınırıyla (PG) gönderilir ve çocuklara yönelik olarak etiketlenmez.

Herhangi bir uygulama içi satın almadan sonra geçiş ve açılış reklamları 7 gün boyunca gösterilmez. "Reklamları Kaldır"ı satın alan, aktif Premium aboneliği olan veya toplamda en az 4,99 ABD doları karşılığı harcama yapan oyuncular artık banner, geçiş ve açılış reklamları görmez. Ayrıntılar: Google, hizmetlerini kullanan site ve uygulamalardan gelen bilgileri nasıl kullanır.
